# Big Fluffies

> This mutant creature is easily spotted since it is huge and pink, possibly arising from some strange things involving a hare, rum, and a Megasloth. The extra fluff makes them sturdy in the cold and their wool provides excellent protection against elements and more.  They're strangely bright and can be trained.  Recent experimentation revealed their thick wool supports holding items and they don't appear to mind or even notice, making them useful as pack animals.

This is a mod for RimWorld that adds an absurd new creature.

*(The version 1.0 of this mod simply used a giant hare for the model and a variety of other lazy shortcuts.  This version is available as a download, but only the new version is maintained on Steam.)*

## What's in the box

- **The fluffies themselves:** huge, trainable, shearable, milkable pack animals that roam in herds. Every tamed fluffy gets a silly name, drawn from a few hundred names and titles (plus a few hundred combined names), e.g. *Reverend Gumdrop the Cloud*, *Hangover McFloof*, or just *Regret*.
- **Personality:** they nuzzle colonists constantly, never go manhunter, track zero filth into your base, and when hurt they don't bleed, they shed fluff everywhere. They're still prey, so predators will try. It rarely goes well for the predator.
- **Strawberry fluff milk** cooks into:
  - **strawberry fluff puffs:** a recreation snack, like chocolate.
  - **strawberry sugar-rush shakes:** faster work and movement for most of a shift, then a crash. Not addictive, and you can schedule them in drug policies.
- **Pink fluffy beds (single and double):** very comfortable, and pawns finish sleeping faster.
- **Pink fluff carpet:** a very beautiful floor.
- **Apparel:** a floof cap (warm, fewer mental breaks), a floof scarf (faster walking) and a fluffherd's robe (better taming, training and gathering).
- **Fluffy plushies:** art objects with their own names and descriptions, some of which talk to the player directly. With [Plushies](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2259582816) active, colonists also cuddle them and sleep better next to them. (These replace the old FluffiesPlushies mod.) Fluffies also occasionally photobomb ordinary sculptures.
- **Fluffy traders** who sell the milk, snacks, shakes and apparel.

Works with both fluffy mods at once. Each mod brings its own colour of everything.
